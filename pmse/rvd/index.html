Imaging methods & references

Experimental methods fit calibrated complex covariances directly, with nonnegative source power. Compact echoes use pixel ℓ₁ and optional reweighting; PMSE uses analysis ℓ₁ on a 2-D Haar pyramid, not TV or point-source sparsity. The image is relative reconstructed power, not calibrated SNR. Sparse censoring, approximate noise weights and historical phase calibration limit interpretation; inspect the residual and convergence warning.

  • Beck & Teboulle (2009): FISTA — accelerated proximal solver for compact-source ℓ₁.
  • Candès, Wakin & Boyd (2008): reweighted ℓ₁ — optional compact-source sparsity refinement.
  • Girard et al. (2015): sparse representations in interferometric imaging — background on multiscale sparse imaging; not a claim of the same implementation.

About the observations & downloads

The download button exports the selected UTC day's available native HDF5 files in a TAR archive, including sparse complex spectra for all seven receiver channels and all retained coherent looks, plus the Digital Metadata properties file. Today's archive grows as observations arrive.

Only cells passing the 3 dB excess-signal SNR and statistical gates are retained. Missing cells are censored, not measured zero power. Grey indicates no delivered record, which may still be processing or transferring. A high radial velocity alone does not establish a vertical draft.

Altitude is the nominal beam-center height. HSV encodes Doppler centroid, spectral width, and peak SNR. Radar images use a selected slant-range shell and at most 120 seconds of looks; their intensity is relative reconstructed power, not calibrated SNR. Historical phase calibration has not been independently validated for these observations.
